50 Christmas Trivia Questions and Answers

Fifty Christmas trivia questions with answers, written for office holiday parties, team events and family quiz nights. They cover decorations, holiday treats, Christmas movies and songs, Santa and his reindeer, winter folklore and Christmas traditions from Canada and around the world.

Questions get harder within each category: the first ones warm everyone up, and the last ones decide who wins. Each answer comes with a short explanation, so the reveal is half the fun. Read them aloud, print them, or play them on a big screen as a team game with scores, a timer and a narrator.

How to use this page: answers are hidden. Read a question, let everyone guess, then press Show answer. Questions 1–25 are easier, 26–50 are harder.

Deck the HallsChristmas tradition trivia · Round One, 100–500 points

Mistletoe, Advent calendars and the Christmas tree: where the season’s best-known customs came from.

  1. 1.

    Under which plant are people traditionally supposed to kiss at Christmas?

    Show answer

    Mistletoe

    The kissing custom was popular in 18th-century England. Traditionally, a berry was picked off for every kiss.

    100 pts
  2. 2.

    What is the English name for the Christmas cake shaped like a log, known in French as bûche de Noël?

    Show answer

    Yule log

    It recalls the old custom of burning a huge log in the hearth over the Christmas season.

    200 pts
  3. 3.

    Prince Albert helped make the decorated Christmas tree popular in Britain. In which country did the tradition begin?

    Show answer

    Germany

    A famous 1848 drawing of Queen Victoria and Albert's family around their tree made it fashionable across the English-speaking world.

    300 pts
  4. 4.

    On what date does the first door of most Advent calendars open?

    Show answer

    December 1

    The church season of Advent starts on a Sunday, but most calendars simply count December 1 to 24.

    400 pts
  5. 5.

    Which 4th-century bishop of Myra, known for secret gift-giving, inspired the figure of Santa Claus?

    Show answer

    Saint Nicholas

    Myra was in what is now Turkey. The Dutch name Sinterklaas turned into Santa Claus in America.

    500 pts

Oh, Canada!Canadian Christmas trivia · Round One, 100–500 points

Christmas from coast to coast, from Santa’s own postal code to the mummers of Newfoundland.

  1. 6.

    Canada Post delivers children's letters to Santa at a special postal code. What is it?

    Show answer

    H0H 0H0

    Volunteer elves have helped Santa answer millions of letters since the program began in 1982.

    100 pts
  2. 7.

    Mummering, when people in costume and disguise visit neighbours at Christmas, is a tradition of which province?

    Show answer

    Newfoundland and Labrador

    Hosts have to guess who the mummers are before offering them a drink and a treat.

    200 pts
  3. 8.

    Which spiced meat pie is a classic dish at the French-Canadian Christmas Eve feast, the réveillon?

    Show answer

    Tourtière

    It is usually made with pork, and sometimes beef or game, seasoned with cloves and cinnamon.

    300 pts
  4. 9.

    Every year Nova Scotia sends a giant Christmas tree to which American city, as thanks for its help after the 1917 Halifax Explosion?

    Show answer

    Boston

    Boston sent doctors, nurses and supplies within hours. The tree lights up Boston Common.

    400 pts
  5. 10.

    The Huron Carol, Canada's oldest Christmas song, was written around 1642 by Jesuit missionary Jean de Brébeuf. In which language?

    Show answer

    Wendat (Huron)

    The English words most people know today were written in 1926 by Jesse Edgar Middleton.

    500 pts

Sweet TreatsChristmas food and candy trivia · Round One, 100–500 points

Candy canes, eggnog, gingerbread and the festive breads of Europe.

  1. 11.

    What flavour is a classic red and white candy cane?

    Show answer

    Peppermint

    Candy canes were plain white at first. The red stripes were added around 1900.

    100 pts
  2. 12.

    Eggnog is made with milk, cream and sugar, plus which main ingredient that gives it its name?

    Show answer

    Eggs

    Many recipes also add nutmeg, and grown-up versions add rum, brandy or bourbon.

    200 pts
  3. 13.

    Gingerbread houses became popular in which country in the 1800s, after the Brothers Grimm published Hansel and Gretel?

    Show answer

    Germany

    In the fairy tale, the witch's house in the forest is made of bread and cake.

    300 pts
  4. 14.

    What is the name of the tall, domed Italian Christmas bread with candied fruit and raisins, first made in Milan?

    Show answer

    Panettone

    Its dough rises slowly over several days, which makes it light and fluffy.

    400 pts
  5. 15.

    Stollen, a fruit bread covered in icing sugar, is the pride of the Christmas market in which German city?

    Show answer

    Dresden

    Dresden's market, the Striezelmarkt, dates back to 1434 and holds a Stollen festival with a giant loaf.

    500 pts

Christmas at the MoviesChristmas movie trivia · Round One, 100–500 points

Holiday classics, from It’s a Wonderful Life to Home Alone and Elf.

  1. 16.

    In Home Alone (1990), which city is the McCallister family flying to when they leave Kevin behind?

    Show answer

    Paris

    Kevin is left alone in the family's house near Chicago.

    100 pts
  2. 17.

    What is the name of Will Ferrell's character, a human raised by elves, in the 2003 film Elf?

    Show answer

    Buddy

    Buddy travels from the North Pole to New York City to find his father.

    200 pts
  3. 18.

    In Dr. Seuss's How the Grinch Stole Christmas, what is the name of the Grinch's dog?

    Show answer

    Max

    The Grinch ties an antler to Max's head to make him look like a reindeer.

    300 pts
  4. 19.

    In It's a Wonderful Life (1946), what is the first name of George Bailey's guardian angel?

    Show answer

    Clarence

    Clarence Odbody is an angel second class who still has to earn his wings.

    400 pts
  5. 20.

    In Miracle on 34th Street (1947), Kris Kringle works as Santa at which New York department store?

    Show answer

    Macy's

    The film opens at the real Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade.

    500 pts

Snow & IceWinter and snow trivia · Round One, 100–500 points

Snowflakes, avalanches, hoarfrost and a famous snowman from Quebec.

  1. 21.

    How many sides or points does a snowflake crystal have?

    Show answer

    Six

    Water molecules freeze into a hexagonal pattern, so every snowflake is built on six.

    100 pts
  2. 22.

    What do we call a large mass of snow that suddenly slides down a mountainside?

    Show answer

    An avalanche

    Western Canada's mountains see thousands of avalanches every winter.

    200 pts
  3. 23.

    What is the name of the snowman mascot of the Quebec Winter Carnival?

    Show answer

    Bonhomme

    Bonhomme Carnaval wears a red toque and a colourful sash called a ceinture fléchée.

    300 pts
  4. 24.

    At what temperature do the Celsius and Fahrenheit scales show the same number?

    Show answer

    Minus 40

    −40 °C is exactly −40 °F, a number many Prairie winters know well.

    400 pts
  5. 25.

    What is the name for the feathery white ice crystals that form on trees and grass on cold, clear nights?

    Show answer

    Hoarfrost

    It forms when water vapour turns straight into ice, skipping the liquid stage.

    500 pts

Santa's WorkshopSanta and reindeer trivia · Round Two, 200–1,000 points

Santa, his reindeer and the stories that made them famous.

  1. 26.

    In the 1823 poem 'A Visit from St. Nicholas', how many reindeer pull Santa's sleigh?

    Show answer

    Eight

    Rudolph came later, in 1939, so he is not in the poem.

    200 pts
  2. 27.

    Which of Santa's reindeer shares its name with the Roman god of love?

    Show answer

    Cupid

    The others in the poem are Dasher, Dancer, Prancer, Vixen, Comet, Donner and Blitzen.

    400 pts
  3. 28.

    In the Netherlands, Sinterklaas arrives in mid-November by steamboat. Which country is he said to sail from?

    Show answer

    Spain

    Dutch children leave out a shoe with a carrot for his horse.

    600 pts
  4. 29.

    Rudolph the Red-Nosed Reindeer was created in 1939 for a free booklet handed out by which department store chain?

    Show answer

    Montgomery Ward

    Copywriter Robert L. May wrote the story. Millions of copies were given away that first Christmas.

    800 pts
  5. 30.

    In the first version of 'A Visit from St. Nicholas', two reindeer were called Dunder and Blixem. What do these Dutch words mean?

    Show answer

    Thunder and lightning

    Later printings changed them to Donder and Blitzen. Today most people say Donner and Blitzen.

    1000 pts

Around the WorldChristmas traditions around the world · Round Two, 200–1,000 points

How other countries celebrate, from Mexico and Japan to Italy and Catalonia.

  1. 31.

    In which country is fried chicken from KFC a popular Christmas dinner?

    Show answer

    Japan

    It began with a 1974 ad campaign. Families often order their Christmas bucket weeks ahead.

    200 pts
  2. 32.

    In Mexico, what are the nine nights of candlelit processions before Christmas called?

    Show answer

    Las Posadas

    From December 16 to 24, people act out Mary and Joseph's search for shelter.

    400 pts
  3. 33.

    Which country gives London a giant Christmas tree for Trafalgar Square every year, as thanks for help in the Second World War?

    Show answer

    Norway

    The tradition started in 1947. The tree comes from the forests around Oslo.

    600 pts
  4. 34.

    In Italy, which kind old witch flies on a broom to bring sweets to children on the night before January 6?

    Show answer

    La Befana

    Good children get sweets and naughty ones get a lump of coal, often made of black sugar.

    800 pts
  5. 35.

    In Catalonia, which smiling wooden log is 'fed' for weeks and then hit with sticks until it gives out sweets?

    Show answer

    Tió de Nadal

    It wears a red cap and a blanket. Children sing to it while they tap it on Christmas Eve or Day.

    1000 pts

Christmas CreaturesChristmas folklore trivia · Round Two, 200–1,000 points

Krampus, the Yule Lads, the Yule Cat and other creatures of winter folklore.

  1. 36.

    Krampus is a horned creature from Alpine folklore who travels with Saint Nicholas. Whom does he punish?

    Show answer

    Naughty children

    Saint Nicholas rewards good children, and Krampus deals with the rest.

    200 pts
  2. 37.

    In Iceland, how many mischievous Yule Lads visit children in the nights before Christmas?

    Show answer

    Thirteen

    They arrive one at a time, starting December 12, and leave gifts or rotten potatoes in shoes.

    400 pts
  3. 38.

    Which giant cat from Icelandic folklore is said to eat people who don't get new clothes before Christmas?

    Show answer

    The Yule Cat (Jólakötturinn)

    The legend encouraged people to finish their wool work before the holidays.

    600 pts
  4. 39.

    Krampusnacht, when people in Krampus costumes parade through Alpine towns, is held on which date?

    Show answer

    December 5

    It is the eve of Saint Nicholas Day, December 6.

    800 pts
  5. 40.

    In a Ukrainian Christmas legend, which creature decorated a poor family's tree overnight, which is why it appears on tree ornaments?

    Show answer

    A spider

    In the story, the spider's webs turned to silver and gold in the morning light, the origin of tinsel.

    1000 pts

Holiday HitsChristmas music trivia · Round Two, 200–1,000 points

Famous Christmas songs and the people behind them. No lyrics required.

  1. 41.

    Who sang the 1994 hit 'All I Want for Christmas Is You'?

    Show answer

    Mariah Carey

    It first reached number one on the US Billboard Hot 100 in 2019, 25 years after its release.

    200 pts
  2. 42.

    Which Canadian singer from Burnaby, B.C., released the hugely popular 2011 album 'Christmas'?

    Show answer

    Michael Bublé

    Its songs return to the charts every December.

    400 pts
  3. 43.

    'Silent Night' was first performed on Christmas Eve 1818 in a small village church in which country?

    Show answer

    Austria

    Joseph Mohr wrote the words and Franz Xaver Gruber the music, in Oberndorf near Salzburg.

    600 pts
  4. 44.

    'White Christmas', one of the best-selling singles of all time, was written by which American songwriter?

    Show answer

    Irving Berlin

    Bing Crosby's 1942 recording has sold tens of millions of copies.

    800 pts
  5. 45.

    In December 1965, astronauts on which NASA mission played 'Jingle Bells' on a harmonica and bells, the first song played in space?

    Show answer

    Gemini 6A

    Wally Schirra and Tom Stafford first joked that they had spotted a UFO in a red suit.

    1000 pts

Christmas HistoryChristmas history trivia · Round Two, 200–1,000 points

Dickens, the first Christmas cards, the first electric tree lights and a Christmas ban.

  1. 46.

    Which 1843 novella by Charles Dickens tells the story of Ebenezer Scrooge?

    Show answer

    A Christmas Carol

    The first printing sold out in days.

    200 pts
  2. 47.

    The first commercial Christmas cards were printed in 1843 in which country?

    Show answer

    England (United Kingdom)

    Sir Henry Cole ordered 1,000 cards because he had no time to write letters by hand.

    400 pts
  3. 48.

    What does the 'X' in 'Xmas' originally stand for?

    Show answer

    The Greek letter chi, for Christ

    Chi, written X, is the first letter of 'Christ' in Greek, so Xmas is centuries old, not a modern shortcut.

    600 pts
  4. 49.

    Electric Christmas tree lights were first shown in 1882 by Edward H. Johnson, a business partner of which inventor?

    Show answer

    Thomas Edison

    Johnson's tree in New York had 80 red, white and blue bulbs.

    800 pts
  5. 50.

    From 1659 to 1681, celebrating Christmas was against the law in the Massachusetts Bay Colony. Which group passed the ban?

    Show answer

    The Puritans

    Anyone caught celebrating could be fined five shillings.

    1000 pts

Bonus: the final questionChristmas Eve round · teams wager points before they see it

In the game, every team secretly wagers part of its score on the category alone, then sees the question. The category is A Christmas Miracle.

  1. 51.

    In December 1914, during the First World War, British and German soldiers on the Western Front stopped fighting, sang carols and met in no man's land. What is this event called?

    Show answer

    The Christmas Truce

    The ceasefire was unofficial. In some places it lasted into the new year.

    wager
  2. 52.

    Tiebreaker: In what year did Gene Autry release his famous recording of 'Rudolph the Red-Nosed Reindeer'?

    Show answer

    1949

    It sold about 1.75 million copies in its first Christmas season.

    closest wins
