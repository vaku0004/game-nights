How a game night works

  1. Open a game on the big screenChrome or Edge on the laptop connected to the TV or projector. Press F11 for full screen.
  2. Name 2 to 4 teamsMix departments, and put remote coworkers on teams with people in the room.
  3. Let the narrator hostTeams pick questions on a game-show board, think for 30 seconds and answer out loud. The game keeps score.
  4. Finish with a wagerOne final question with secret bets, then a podium for the winning team. About an hour in total.

Questions

How much do the games cost?

The games on this site can be played for free right now: open a game, name your teams and play, with no account and no ads. Some games, question packs or custom office editions may become paid later, and anything paid will be clearly marked.

What do we need?

A computer with Chrome or Edge, a big screen or projector and speakers. Players only need their voices, and paper and pens for the final round.

Can remote coworkers play?

Yes. Share the game in Teams, Zoom or Google Meet with computer sound on, and mix remote players into teams with people in the room. Each game has a page on how to set it up.

How many people can play?

2 to 4 teams, ideally 6 to 20 people. With a bigger group, make bigger teams.

Can I change the questions?

Yes. Every game has a question editor, so you can add questions about your own office.
