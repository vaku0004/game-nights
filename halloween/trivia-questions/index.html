50 Halloween Trivia Questions and Answers

Fifty Halloween trivia questions with answers, written for office parties, team events and family quiz nights. They cover classic monsters, horror movies, candy, witches, creepy creatures and Halloween traditions from Canada and around the world.

Questions get harder within each category: the first ones warm everyone up, and the last ones decide who wins. Each answer comes with a short explanation, so the reveal is half the fun. Read them aloud, print them, or play them on a big screen as a team game with scores, a timer and a narrator.

How to use this page: answers are hidden. Read a question, let everyone guess, then press Show answer. Questions 1–25 are easier, 26–50 are harder.

Monster MashClassic monster trivia · Round One, 100–500 points

Dracula, Frankenstein, Jekyll and Hyde: questions about the monsters that started it all.

  1. 1.

    In Bram Stoker's 1897 novel, which count travels from Transylvania to England?

    Show answer

    Count Dracula

    Stoker's count sails to Whitby, England, looking for new victims.

    100 pts
  2. 2.

    What is the first name of Dr. Frankenstein in Mary Shelley's novel?

    Show answer

    Victor

    The monster itself never gets a name.

    200 pts
  3. 3.

    In Robert Louis Stevenson's story, Dr. Jekyll's evil alter ego is Mr. ___?

    Show answer

    Hyde

    Jekyll drinks a potion that turns him into the cruel Edward Hyde.

    300 pts
  4. 4.

    According to legend, a bullet made of which metal can stop a werewolf?

    Show answer

    Silver

    Silver has long been linked to purity and protection from evil, and werewolf films made the silver bullet famous.

    400 pts
  5. 5.

    Ancient Egyptians stored a mummy's organs in special containers. What are these jars called?

    Show answer

    Canopic jars

    The jars held the stomach, intestines, lungs and liver. The heart stayed inside the body.

    500 pts

Pumpkin PatchPumpkin and jack-o’-lantern trivia · Round One, 100–500 points

Where jack-o’-lanterns came from, and what a pumpkin really is.

  1. 6.

    Botanically speaking, is a pumpkin a fruit or a vegetable?

    Show answer

    A fruit

    It grows from a flower and holds seeds, so botanists call it a fruit.

    100 pts
  2. 7.

    Before pumpkins, people in Ireland and Scotland carved lanterns out of which root vegetable?

    Show answer

    Turnips

    Irish and Scottish immigrants switched to pumpkins in North America, which were bigger and easier to carve. Rutabagas count too.

    200 pts
  3. 8.

    In the Irish legend of "Stingy Jack", who did Jack trick?

    Show answer

    The Devil

    Jack tricked the Devil, so heaven and hell both turned him away. He wanders with a coal inside a carved turnip.

    300 pts
  4. 9.

    What is the Spanish name for hulled pumpkin seeds, a popular snack?

    Show answer

    Pepitas

    Short for "pepitas de calabaza", meaning little squash seeds. They are popular in Mexican cooking.

    400 pts
  5. 10.

    Pumpkin pie spice usually mixes cinnamon, ginger, cloves and which spice that originally came from the Banda Islands?

    Show answer

    Nutmeg

    Nutmeg comes from a tree native to the Banda Islands in Indonesia, once the world's only source.

    500 pts

Spooky CanadaCanadian Halloween trivia · Round One, 100–500 points

Halloween from coast to coast, from orange UNICEF boxes to a haunted hotel in the Rockies.

  1. 11.

    For decades, Canadian kids carried small orange boxes while trick-or-treating to collect coins for which organization?

    Show answer

    UNICEF

    UNICEF Canada started the program in 1955 and retired the boxes in 2006.

    100 pts
  2. 12.

    The earliest known record of kids in English-speaking North America going door to door on Halloween is a 1911 newspaper report from which Ontario city?

    Show answer

    Kingston

    Kids went "guising" and got nuts and candy for songs and rhymes.

    200 pts
  3. 13.

    Which wafer chocolate bar, a Canadian favourite, has a coffee-flavoured filling?

    Show answer

    Coffee Crisp

    Layers of vanilla wafer and foamed coffee-flavoured candy in milk chocolate, made by Nestlé in Toronto.

    300 pts
  4. 14.

    Legend says a ghostly bellman named Sam still helps guests at which castle-like hotel in the Rockies?

    Show answer

    The Fairmont Banff Springs

    Guests say he helps with stuck doors and directions, then vanishes. The legend is based on a real bellman.

    400 pts
  5. 15.

    For about twenty years from 1999, people in Windsor, Nova Scotia raced across a lake sitting in what?

    Show answer

    Giant hollowed-out pumpkins

    The Windsor Pumpkin Regatta on Lake Pisiquid used giant pumpkins from the Dill family farm. It ended when the lake was drained.

    500 pts

    The 1993 stop-motion film, produced by Tim Burton, is set in Halloween Town.

  4. 19.

    In which 1982 film does a little girl turn to the TV and announce "They're here"?

    Show answer

    Poltergeist

    Little Carol Anne says it to the static on the family's TV screen.

    400 pts
  5. 20.

    In "Ghostbusters" (1984), which giant creature stomps through New York in the finale?

PhobiasFear and phobia trivia · Round One, 100–500 points

Name the fear. Some of these words are scarier than the fears themselves.

  1. 21.

    What is the fear of spiders called?

    Show answer

    Arachnophobia

    From the Greek word "arachne", spider.

    100 pts
  2. 22.

    What is the fear of the number 13 called?

    Show answer

    Triskaidekaphobia

    From Greek: "tris" (three), "kai" (and), "deka" (ten).

    200 pts
  3. 23.

    "Nomophobia" is the fear of being without what?

    Show answer

    Your mobile phone

    Short for "no mobile phone phobia".

    300 pts
  4. 24.

    "Coulrophobia" is the fear of what?

    Show answer

    Clowns

    Often traced to a Greek word for a stilt-walker.

    400 pts
  5. 25.

    What is the name for the fear of Halloween?

    Show answer

    Samhainophobia

    Named after Samhain, the Celtic festival that Halloween grew from.

    500 pts

Witches & WizardsWitch trivia · Round Two, 200–1,000 points

Witches in history, literature, film and folklore.

  1. 26.

    What do witches traditionally fly on?

    Show answer

    A broomstick

    Old folk tales and woodcuts showed witches riding brooms to secret gatherings.

    200 pts
  2. 27.

    In 1692, a famous series of witch trials took place in which Massachusetts town?

    Show answer

    Salem

    About 200 people were accused, and 20 were executed.

    400 pts
  3. 28.

    How many witches appear at the start of Shakespeare's "Macbeth"?

    Show answer

    Three

    The Weird Sisters predict that Macbeth will become king.

    600 pts
  4. 29.

    In "The Wizard of Oz" (1939), what makes the Wicked Witch of the West melt?

    Show answer

    A bucket of water

    Dorothy throws water to put out a fire on the Scarecrow and splashes the witch.

    800 pts
  5. 30.

    In Slavic folklore, which witch lives in a hut that stands on chicken legs?

    Show answer

    Baba Yaga

    In the tales, Baba Yaga flies in a mortar and steers with a pestle.

    1000 pts

Candy CrushHalloween candy trivia · Round Two, 200–1,000 points

The stories behind the treats in a trick-or-treat bag, including a few Canadian favourites.

  1. 31.

    Name the three colours of classic candy corn.

    Show answer

    White, orange and yellow

    From the tip down: white, orange, then a yellow base.

    200 pts
  2. 32.

    The Snickers bar was named after what kind of animal owned by the Mars family?

    Show answer

    A horse

    The Mars family named the bar after their favourite horse, Snickers, around 1930.

    400 pts
  3. 33.

    The bar we now call Kit Kat was launched in 1935 under a different name. In which country was it made?

    Show answer

    England (United Kingdom)

    Rowntree's of York sold it as "Chocolate Crisp". It became Kit Kat in 1937.

    600 pts
  4. 34.

    In Canada, Smarties are candy-coated chocolates. What are Smarties in the United States?

    Show answer

    Tart sugar tablets sold in rolls

    Canadian Smarties are made by Nestlé. The American candy with the same name is a completely different sweet.

    800 pts
  5. 35.

    This New Brunswick company, Canada's oldest candy maker (founded 1873), says it made the first wrapped chocolate bar in North America. Name it.

    Show answer

    Ganong

    Ganong Bros. of St. Stephen, New Brunswick, still makes chocolate today, including the Pal-o-Mine bar.

    1000 pts

Creatures of the NightBats, owls and spiders · Round Two, 200–1,000 points

Real animals with a spooky reputation.

  1. 36.

    Which flying mammal finds its way in the dark using echolocation?

    Show answer

    The bat

    Bats make high-pitched calls and listen for the echoes to find insects in the dark.

    200 pts
  2. 37.

    What is a group of owls called?

    Show answer

    A parliament

    Owls usually live alone, but a group of them is called a parliament.

    400 pts
  3. 38.

    Which spider is known for a red hourglass mark on its belly?

    Show answer

    The black widow

    The red hourglass is on the underside of the female's abdomen.

    600 pts
  4. 39.

    Vampire bats really exist. What do they feed on?

    Show answer

    Blood

    Usually from livestock and birds. They lap it up rather than suck it.

    800 pts
  5. 40.

    About how many degrees can an owl turn its head?

    Show answer

    About 270 degrees

    Owls have twice as many neck bones as people, so they can look over their backs.

    1000 pts

Around the WorldHalloween traditions around the world · Round Two, 200–1,000 points

Where Halloween came from, and how other countries mark the season.

  1. 41.

    Which Mexican holiday on November 1–2 honours loved ones who have died?

    Show answer

    Día de los Muertos (Day of the Dead)

    Families build altars called ofrendas with photos, marigolds and the favourite foods of the dead.

    200 pts
  2. 42.

    Halloween grew out of which ancient Celtic festival?

    Show answer

    Samhain

    Samhain marked the end of the harvest, when the line to the spirit world was believed to grow thin.

    400 pts
  3. 43.

    Decorated sugar skulls made for the Day of the Dead have what Spanish name?

    Show answer

    Calaveras (de azúcar)

    "Calavera" is Spanish for skull. The sugar skulls often carry the name of a loved one.

    600 pts
  4. 44.

    On what date does Britain celebrate Guy Fawkes Night with bonfires and fireworks?

    Show answer

    November 5

    It marks the failed Gunpowder Plot of 1605 to blow up the Houses of Parliament.

    800 pts
  5. 45.

    The word "Halloween" is a shortened form of which phrase?

    Show answer

    All Hallows' Eve

    "Hallow" is an old word for saint. October 31 is the evening before All Saints' Day.

    1000 pts

Tougher film questions for horror fans.

    The Sanderson sisters cursed Thackery in 1693 for trying to save his little sister.

    600 pts
  4. 49.

    Which Stephen King novel features the clown Pennywise?

    Show answer

    It

    Stephen King published "It" in 1986. Pennywise returns to Derry, Maine, every 27 years.

    800 pts
  5. 50.

    What is the name of the hotel in "The Shining"?

    Show answer

    The Overlook Hotel

    Jack Torrance becomes the winter caretaker of the Overlook, high in the Colorado Rockies.

    1000 pts

  1. 51.

    Bram Stoker borrowed Dracula's name from a 15th-century ruler of Wallachia who was infamous for punishing enemies on wooden stakes. What was this ruler's name?

    Show answer

    Vlad the Impaler

    Also known as Vlad III, or Vlad Dracula. "Dracula" means "son of the dragon".

    Tiebreaker: In what year was Bram Stoker's novel "Dracula" first published?
