Privacy

This site uses Google Analytics to count visits and to see which parts of the games are used, for example how many games are started and finished. It does not collect team names, answers or anything typed into the question editor, and it does not use advertising cookies.

For visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and Quebec, analytics cookies are turned off and Google receives only cookieless signals. You can also block analytics with any browser privacy setting or extension; the games work the same.

Each game saves its progress, your edited questions and voice settings in your own browser (local storage) so you can continue after closing the tab. That information never leaves your computer.

The games have no accounts and no sign-up, and nothing is sold or shared.
