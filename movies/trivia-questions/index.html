50 Movie Trivia Questions and Answers

Fifty movie trivia questions with answers, written for team events, office game nights and movie nights at home. They cover animated classics, superheroes, food on screen, famous lines, film series, filmmaking, directors, movie music, Hollywood history and world cinema.

Questions get harder within each category: the first ones warm everyone up, and the last ones decide who wins. Each answer comes with a short explanation, so the reveal is half the fun. Read them aloud, print them, or play them on a big screen as a team game with scores, a timer and a narrator.

How to use this page: answers are hidden. Read a question, let everyone guess, then press Show answer. Questions 1–25 are easier, 26–50 are harder.

Animated ClassicsAnimated movie trivia · Round One, 100–500 points

Nemo, Olaf, Buzz Lightyear, Spirited Away and Disney’s first feature film.

  1. 1.

    In 'Finding Nemo' (2003), what kind of fish is Nemo?

    Show answer

    A clownfish

    Real clownfish live among the stinging tentacles of sea anemones, which protect them.

    100 pts
  2. 2.

    What is the name of the friendly snowman in Disney's 'Frozen' (2013)?

    Show answer

    Olaf

    He dreams of summer, not knowing what heat does to snow.

    200 pts
  3. 3.

    In 'Toy Story' (1995), what is the name of the space ranger toy?

    Show answer

    Buzz Lightyear

    'Toy Story' was the first feature film made entirely with computer animation.

    300 pts
  4. 4.

    Which Japanese animated film by Hayao Miyazaki won the Oscar for Best Animated Feature in 2003?

    Show answer

    Spirited Away

    Miyazaki won the award again in 2024, for 'The Boy and the Heron'.

    400 pts
  5. 5.

    What was Walt Disney's first full-length animated feature film, released in 1937?

    Show answer

    Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs

    Many in Hollywood called it 'Disney's Folly' before it became a huge hit.

    500 pts

Movie FoodMovie food trivia · Round One, 100–500 points

Ratatouille, popcorn, golden tickets, a box of chocolates and breakfast on Fifth Avenue.

  1. 11.

    In 'Ratatouille' (2007), what kind of animal dreams of becoming a great chef in Paris?

    Show answer

    A rat

    His name is Remy, and he cooks by steering a young man's hair from under his hat.

    100 pts
  2. 12.

    Which snack became a movie-theater favorite in the United States during the Great Depression of the 1930s?

    Show answer

    Popcorn

    It was cheap, filling and easy to sell, and it helped many theaters survive.

    200 pts
  3. 13.

    In 'Willy Wonka & the Chocolate Factory' (1971), what does Charlie find in his chocolate bar?

    Show answer

    A golden ticket

    Only five golden tickets were hidden in chocolate bars around the world.

    300 pts
  4. 14.

    In 'Forrest Gump' (1994), what does Forrest's mother say life is like?

    Show answer

    A box of chocolates

    Because you never know what you're going to get.

    400 pts
  5. 15.

    In 'Breakfast at Tiffany's' (1961), what does Audrey Hepburn's character eat while looking into the jewelry store's window?

    Show answer

    A pastry (a Danish) and coffee

    The opening scene was filmed early in the morning on Fifth Avenue in New York.

    500 pts

Behind the ScenesFilmmaking trivia · Round Two, 200–1,000 points

Clapperboards, CGI, green screens, cinematographers and the first talkie.

  1. 26.

    What is the name of the hinged board snapped shut at the start of each shot?

    Show answer

    A clapperboard (slate)

    The snap helps editors match the sound to the picture.

    200 pts
  2. 27.

    In filmmaking, what does 'CGI' stand for?

    Show answer

    Computer-generated imagery

    'Jurassic Park' (1993) was a landmark for lifelike CGI creatures.

    400 pts
  3. 28.

    What color is the screen most often used behind actors, so backgrounds can be added later?

    Show answer

    Green

    Green is used because it is a color rarely found in skin tones and most costumes.

    600 pts
  4. 29.

    What job title is given to the person in charge of the camera and lighting, also called the cinematographer?

    Show answer

    Director of photography

    On set, the role is often shortened to 'DP'.

    800 pts
  5. 30.

    Which 1927 film was the first feature-length 'talkie' with synchronized dialogue?

    Show answer

    The Jazz Singer

    Before it, feature films were silent, with live music in the theater.

    1000 pts

Play these questions as a team game

Every question on this page is part of Victoria’s Movie Night, a free quiz game for the big screen. Instead of reading from a list, teams pick categories and point values on a game-show board and race the clock.

  • Scores and turnsThe board keeps the score and shows whose turn it is. A wrong answer costs the points.
  • A narratorA movie-trailer voice reads every question and answer aloud, so the host can play too.
  • 30-second timerTeams talk it over, then answer. The game keeps moving.
  • Two surprisesThe Plot Twist and the Popcorn Bucket hide on the board.
  • Final CutSecret wagers, one last question and a podium for the team that steals the show.
  • Your own questionsChange any question or answer right in the game, for example to add questions about your office.
